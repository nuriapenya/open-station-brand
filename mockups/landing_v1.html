Mission control for your WordPress.

Windows

Drag, resize, minimize, snap, tile. Moderate comments while the editor stays open. Radical, we know.

Dock & taskbar

The admin menu, reborn as a dock. Everything you have open, one glance away in the taskbar.

Spaces

Virtual desktops for different missions. Writing on one, plugin wrangling on another, chaos on a third.

Command palette

Hit ⌘K and teleport. The optional AI assistant finds that post you wrote in 2019.

Session restore

Reload the browser, reboot the planet: your windows come back exactly as you left them.

Content Graph

Your content as a living star map. Watch posts, pages, and media orbit each other.
