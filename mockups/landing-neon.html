/// why_windows

Work side by side,
not one page at a time.

[ 01 ]

Everything_at_once

Edit a post while the Media Library sits beside it and the comments queue floats in the corner. Real work uses more than one screen.

[ 02 ]

It_remembers

Close the tab. Come back tomorrow. Every window reopens exactly where you left it: same position, same size, same Space.

[ 03 ]

Nothing_breaks

Zero core patches, no external dependencies, fully opt-in per user. Turn it off and classic wp-admin is right where you left that, too.

/// system_capabilities · 08 items

Everything a
desktop should do.

01

Window_manager

Every admin screen opens as a draggable, resizable window. Drag, snap, tile, minimize, maximize. Plugin pages included.

02

Dock_+_taskbar

The admin menu becomes a dock of icons, with a taskbar that keeps every open window one click away.

03

Spaces

Virtual desktops for different jobs. Writing on one, moderation on another, site settings on a third.

04

Session_restore

Reload the browser and the whole desktop comes back exactly as you left it. No more starting over.

05

Desktop_files

Drag posts, media, and links onto the wallpaper. Group them into folders, like a desktop that earns the name.

06

Command_palette

Cmd+K opens screens and content instantly, with an optional AI assistant that searches your site.

AI needs WP 7.0+
07

Content_graph

An interactive canvas map of your content and how it all connects, drawn live from your site.

08

Cross-window_drag

Pick something up in one window and drop it in another. Media into posts, posts into folders.
