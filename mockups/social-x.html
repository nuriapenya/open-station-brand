Social kit · X · Snap

Two takes on Snap at 1500 × 500, the 3:1 format X still uses in 2026, with the round holo chip as the avatar. Same skeleton in both: Void and stars, the lockup and two lines of mono at the left, the product at the right. What changes is which moment of the product is on show, from loosest to most literal. X renders the header at 600 × 200 on desktop and around 350px wide on a phone, so both are built to hold up small: big shapes, few of them, and the bottom left kept clear for the avatar.

Export at 400 × 400 for X. The artwork is the same either way, so the only decision per platform is whether the crop is a circle or a rounded square. Both keep the mark on Void, which is what the chip rule asks for.
