Miomesh loading spinners

Six spinner explorations built on the Miomesh gradient (Pulse #E94FF3 through #AA67FF and #A580FF into #4B3EFF), for transitions between screens and for loading content. Each one floats over a cosmo shadow: a soft nebula glow pooled beneath the mark. Toggle it off to compare, and switch the stage surface to check contrast.

1. Halo quiet default

The full Miomesh sweep as a ring, with a Pulse head and a tail that fades into deep blue. The workhorse: content loading anywhere in the shell.

2. Comet nods to the Mio comet

A bright Pulse head with a short trail, circling fast. More playful than Halo; suits opening a window or fetching a screen.

3. Orbit the station itself

A bright core with two moons counter-rotating on faint orbit lines. The most literal take on the space territory; fits bigger moments like first boot of the desktop.

Starting station

4. Corona Halo, quieter

Halo at hairline weight, turning slower, with no head and a tail that fades further into the dark. For background work that should not pull the eye, and for small inline spots where Halo is too loud.

Syncing

5. Relay simplest

Three dots in Miomesh order, handing the light along. The plainest thing in the set: three elements, one keyframe, and it stays legible at any size. Good for inline status next to a label.

Indexing content

6. Warp for Spaces transitions

Rings leaving the center one after another, each carrying the next stop of the ramp, so the sequence cools from Pulse into deep blue. An arrival pulse: made for switching Spaces or landing on a new screen.

Entering space

All CSS, no JavaScript. Every spinner is markup plus a keyframe, and the two toggles above are radio inputs read with :has(), so this page ships no script at all. Shown at 56px on the stage and 24px inline.

Only transform and opacity animate, which keeps each spinner on the compositor and off the main thread: no animated colors, shadows, or filters anywhere. The cosmo shadow is two static blurred layers behind the mark, rasterized once, so the glow costs nothing per frame.

Accessibility. Each stage is a role="status" region labelled with what is loading ("Loading widgets", not "spinner"), and every shape inside is aria-hidden, so a screen reader hears the state and none of the decoration. Under prefers-reduced-motion nothing rotates or travels; the mark fades slowly in place instead. Nothing blinks faster than once a second, well inside the flash threshold.

Contrast. On Void and Obsidian the whole ramp clears 3:1 against the surface (Pulse 6.4:1, deep blue 3.2:1). On Mist the cool end still passes at 4.8:1 but Pulse drops to 2.4:1, so on light surfaces the spinner should lead with the blue half of the ramp rather than the pink.
