Miomesh loading spinners

Six spinner explorations built on the Miomesh gradient (Pulse #F252FC through #AA67FF and #A580FF into #4B3EFF), for transitions between screens and for loading content. Each one floats over a cosmo shadow: a soft nebula glow pooled beneath the mark. Toggle it off to compare, and switch the stage surface to check contrast.

v1 Halo quiet default

The full Miomesh sweep as a ring, with a Pulse head and a tail that fades into deep blue. The workhorse: content loading anywhere in the shell.

Loading widgets

v2 Comet nods to the Mio comet

A bright Pulse head with a short trail, circling fast. More playful than Halo; suits opening a window or fetching a screen.

Opening window

v3 Orbit the station itself

A Starlight core with two moons counter-rotating on faint orbit lines. The most literal take on the space territory; fits bigger moments like first boot of the desktop.

Starting station

v4 Mio mascot territory

A ghost blob breathing in Miomesh, drifting the way Mio does between windows. Kept abstract since Mio's final form is still open; made for AI assistant thinking states.

Mio is thinking

v5 Docking instrument voice

Twelve ticks chasing around the ring, each holding its own stop of the gradient. Reads as a docking sequence; pairs with the mono voice in the palette and system operations.

Indexing content

v6 Warp for Spaces transitions

Rings arriving from the center, cooling from Pulse into deep blue as they expand. An arrival pulse: made for switching Spaces or landing on a new screen.

Entering space

All variants are CSS-only except the Docking ticks, which are generated in a few lines of JS; everything animates transform and opacity, so it stays cheap. Shown at 56px (stage) and roughly 24px (inline). In the plugin, the live element should carry role="status" and an aria-label such as "Loading", with the spinner itself aria-hidden. Under prefers-reduced-motion the spinning stops and the mark pulses gently instead.
