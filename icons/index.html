OpenStation icon inventory

Every icon in the plugin, the brand kit and the landing page. 176 marks in all.

Core: Dashicons in use 104Live (shipped with plugin)

104 of the 350 Dashicons WordPress ships. These come from wp-admin: admin-menu registrations, post types, media types. Not ours to change, and out of scope. Swapping them would make the shell disagree with the admin pages inside its own windows.

OpenStation: App and tile icons 12Live (shipped with plugin)

Hand-drawn for the shell on a 64×64 grid, in currentColor so the dock, title bars and desktop themes can tint them. Trash carries two states.
